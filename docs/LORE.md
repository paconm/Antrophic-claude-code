# World and story bible (short)

## The world of Aethra
Three hundred years ago the Sundering War tore Aethra apart. Caedric, the Hollow King (once the Weightless Prince), called the Crowns chains and the First Dragons jailers. He swallowed three dragons and emptied whole forests and cities into Husks: grey, hollow bodies that eat light, wind and warmth. He was sealed, and the Seven Crowns were scattered so that no one could hold them all. Gravity died with the war. Now the black moon rises again, and gravity has returned in one farm boy.

Arcanum Academy floats above the Lake of Seven in the centre of the continent. Every nation sends students to the Rite of Renewal.

## Playable heroes (all adults)
- Ren: human from Emberfall on the Pyrrhos border. Gravity 97%, a hidden Void affinity. The Crystal of Seven turned black at his touch.
- Lyra Windsong: elf of the Silver Court, 61 (a young adult for elves). Wind and Light. Granddaughter of Archon Elowen.
- Torvin Deepforge: dwarf smith, 42. Earth and Fire.
- Kaida Voss: rooftop runner from Sparkhaven, 19. Lightning and Water. Protects her little brother Nico.
- Nerissa Deepcurrent: merfolk heir of the Tide Courts, 20. Water and Light.
- Pip Glimmerwing: fairy of the Oracle's Hall, 120 (adult). Light and Wind.

## Other characters
Headmistress Seraphine (elf), Kael Ashford (Pyrrhos rival, later ally), Professor Vale (traitor), Maren, Archon Elowen, Thalion Windsong, Captain Ysolde, Bramble. Mira is Ren's 11-year-old sister and Nico is Kaida's little brother: they are children and never part of romance.

## Power system
- Ranks F to S, Awakening I to III, a trait and a class per hero, the golden Tree of Life skill tree.
- Bonds with companions unlock arts and change the plot. Romance is optional and gentle.
- Legendary orbs: five per nation, from Lesser to Transcendent, 35 in total. Examples: Weight, Orbit, Horizon, Chronos and Keystone for Gravemont. See `shared/gear.js` for all of them.
- Transcendence: a bearer of all five orbs of one nation steps beyond human.
- Ascension (future volumes): transcended heroes can become gods of their element. Two sealed elements stand outside the seven: Void, which the Hollow King swallowed, and Divine, unheld since the First Dragons.

## Enemies
Husks, shades, wardens, golems, sentinels, the Hollow Knight, and behind them the Hollow King.
